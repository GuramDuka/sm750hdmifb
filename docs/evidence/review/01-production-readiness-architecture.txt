VERDICT: production ready with conditions.
No blocker was found. A plausible blocker was investigated and refuted (see
"Claims checked and refuted"). Three conditions must be met before calling it
production ready: make the EDID read non-blocking, decide explicitly whether
suspend/resume is supported (test it or remove the path), and stop reporting a
successful commit when the hardware mode set failed.

Single most important change to make first: remove the 3 s sleep from the EDID
read path (src/sm750_drm.c:1106, reached from sm750_connector_get_modes at
:1148 under mode_config.mutex/connection_mutex through the 10 s polled detect at
:4620). It is the only defect that can stall every KMS client on the machine,
and it is a small, contained change.

===============================================================
1. KERNEL API CURRENCY (kernel floor 6.17, host 7.3.4)
Score 7/10 - the modern constructs are all present (drmm_mode_config_init,
devm_drm_dev_alloc, drm_client_setup, drm_gem_fb_create, damage clips, CRC via
drm_vblank_work); the version-compatibility story is wrong in detail and the
funcs-table override trades a helper guarantee for CRC.
===============================================================

Verified good:
- drm_client_setup(&sdev->drm, drm_format_info(DRM_FORMAT_XRGB8888)) at
  src/sm750_drm.c:5266 is the current fbdev-client API; no fb_ops, no
  drm_fbdev_* shim. CONFIG_DRM_CLIENT_SETUP=y on the host [K].
- drmm_mode_config_init at :4601 and drm_mode_config_reset at :4680, inside
  sm750_modeset_init (:4593-4695), are the right lifecycle for a drmm-owned
  config [S].
- devm_drm_dev_alloc at :5050 plus devm actions for regions, both iomaps and
  the shadow workqueue (:4712-4720) [S].
- DRM_GEM_SHMEM path uses DRM_FBDEV_SHMEM_DRIVER_OPS and
  DRM_GEM_SIMPLE_DISPLAY_PIPE_SHADOW_PLANE_FUNCS (:4320); this is the modern
  tiny-driver shape [S].
- DRM_VRAM_HELPER (DRM_FBDEV_TTM_DRIVER_OPS, drmm_vram_helper_init at :5238) is
  legacy-adjacent but still maintained and still used by mgag200-class drivers;
  it is not deprecated, just not the direction of travel [K/U].

Findings:
- K1 [minor] The >= 7.0 split rests on a premise the headers contradict.
  src/sm750_drm.c:4301 and :4316 guard the simple-pipe enable_vblank /
  disable_vblank hooks with LINUX_VERSION_CODE < KERNEL_VERSION(7,0,0), yet
  /usr/src/kernels/7.3.4-100.fc43.x86_64/include/drm/drm_simple_kms_helper.h:41-42
  still declares both hooks and the built kernel still contains
  drm_simple_kms_crtc_enable_vblank (System.map:112515) [K]. The guard is
  therefore unnecessary on every kernel in the supported range, and it is the
  reason the driver installs its own CRTC funcs table [S].
- K2 [minor] sm750_crtc_funcs (src/sm750_drm.c:4324-4340, installed at :4633) is
  a full replacement of the helper's CRTC funcs. Its only added capability is
  CRC (get_crc_sources/verify_crc_source/set_crc_source at :4337-4339); it
  re-declares everything else. It does NOT lose the helper's CRTC atomic_check
  (that callback lives in drm_crtc_helper_funcs, i.e. crtc->helper_private,
  which the driver never assigns - verified by grep for helper_private in src/
  and drm_modeset_helper_vtables.h:274 [K]). The cost is maintenance risk, not
  behaviour.
- K3 [minor] 14 LINUX_VERSION_CODE uses, all in src/sm750_drm.c. Acceptable out
  of tree; a hard reject upstream.
- K4 [info] .driver.pm = pm_sleep_ptr(&sm750_pm_ops) at :5373 and
  DEFINE_SIMPLE_DEV_PM_OPS at :5358 are the current idiom [S].
- K5 [info] drm_dev_unregister is not called; drm_dev_unplug at :5303 performs
  the unregister and devm_drm_dev_alloc owns the final put. This is the correct
  modern remove sequence, not an omission. A52 has no cause in this path.

===============================================================
2. ATOMIC/KMS CORRECTNESS AGAINST THE DRM UAPI
Score 6/10 - wiring and helper usage are conventional and correct, but a failed
hardware enable is reported to userspace as success, which is a UAPI-level lie.
===============================================================

- Plane/CRTC/encoder/connector: one primary plane plus a cursor plane
  (ARGB8888, DRM_PLANE_TYPE_CURSOR, PREMULTI blend, :4636-4664), one CRTC, one
  encoder, one HDMIA connector (:4614). Only DRM_FORMAT_XRGB8888 on the primary
  (:4595). This is coherent for the hardware [S].
- Mode config funcs: fb_create=drm_gem_fb_create, atomic_check=
  drm_atomic_helper_check, atomic_commit=drm_atomic_helper_commit
  (:4350-4361); the shadow variant uses drm_gem_fb_create_with_dirty [S].
- Property exposure: damage clips are enabled only for the shadow personality
  (:4631); fb_modifiers_not_supported = 1 (:4606); preferred_depth 24 and
  prefer_shadow 1 (:4611-4612). No connector EDID property attach call, yet the
  EDID blob is published (drm_connector_update_edid_property at :1129) and
  userspace reads it; the property evidently comes from the core, so this is
  not a finding [S/U].
- vblank: synthetic hrtimer, no hardware vblank IRQ. enable/disable are wired
  through sm750_crtc_enable_vblank/sm750_crtc_disable_vblank (:4335-4336) on
  >=7.0 and through the simple-pipe hooks on <7.0. drm_crtc_vblank_on/off are
  called from pipe_enable/pipe_disable [S].
- Page flip: drm_atomic_helper_page_flip (:4331) plus the driver's own event
  arming in pipe_update/pipe_enable (sm750_arm_vblank_event at :3070). The
  driver NULLs crtc_state->event itself, which is the common pattern for
  simple-pipe drivers that need a specific arm point [S].
- Error atomicity - finding A1 [major]: sm750_pipe_enable is void (simple-pipe
  API) and on failure logs "failed to enable HDMI mode: %d at stage %s (commit
  finished, event consumed)" (:4158), dumps four registers and then calls
  drm_crtc_vblank_on, sm750_crc_resume and sm750_arm_vblank_event and returns
  (:4159-4190). The commit therefore completes successfully and the flip event
  fires while the panel is dark. This is a deliberate A27 workaround (avoid a
  stalled commit), and GAP-B8 shows it has occurred in the field (once in 446
  mode sets). A production driver should at least surface the state to
  userspace (sysfs/debugfs flag, and a connector "link" or "self_refresh"
  style property) so a compositor can retry; upstream would ask for the failure
  to be prevented at atomic_check time instead.
- [minor] pipe_update runs before pipe_enable on a modeset commit because there
  is no custom commit_tail. The driver documents and handles this (:4198-4206),
  and shadow uploads are staged before the CRTC turns on; correct but fragile.
- [info] fb_modifiers_not_supported = 1 (:4606) is a justified driver-side
  workaround for KWin submitting a modifier it never advertised; the comment
  block at :4342-4350 records the reason.

===============================================================
3. MODE SETTING, EDID AND DDC
Score 5/10 - mode validation is real, but EDID handling is hand-rolled,
truncated and, in the polled path, able to sleep for seconds.
===============================================================

- Modes come from two places: the sink's EDID (drm_add_edid_modes at :1160)
  when the read succeeds, and four hardcoded catalogs (sm750_standard_modes,
  sm750_custom_modes, sm750_wide_modes, sm750_safe_1920_modes) when it does not
  or when edid_only=0 (:1173-1183) [S]. edid_only defaults to 1 through
  -DSM750_DRM_DEFAULT_EDID_ONLY in src/Makefile:12.
- Mode validation is real: sm750_mode_valid rejects interlace and doublescan,
  enforces hs/vs ordering, htotal/vtotal sanity, clock and width/height limits,
  requires sm750_calc_pll_value to succeed, and checks VRAM fit
  (src/sm750_drm.c:1281-1325; the VRAM estimate at :1319 uses hdisplay*4) [S].
  This is the strongest part of the mode story.
- Detection is polled, not interrupt driven: connector->polled =
  DRM_CONNECTOR_POLL_CONNECT | DRM_CONNECTOR_POLL_DISCONNECT (:4620-4621), so
  a 10 s helper poll with no HPD IRQ. Acceptable for this board today (the
  SiI902x HPD line is not wired to the SM750's interrupt path in this driver);
  upstream would use the bridge's HPD interrupt instead [S].
- detect() returns connected when either the link is up or the receiver sense
  is high (:1222-1245); a sink that is powered but whose DDC is dead is
  therefore "connected" and takes the get_modes path.

Findings:
- E1 [major] 3 s sleep inside the polled probe path. sm750_connector_read_edid
  does two attempts with msleep(3000) between them (src/sm750_drm.c:1106), and
  it is called from sm750_connector_get_modes (:1148), which
  drm_helper_probe_single_connector_modes calls with mode_config.mutex and
  connection_mutex held. With a 10 s poll, an unreadable EDID costs 3 s (or 6 s
  if the outer retry also runs) of a system-wide KMS stall every poll period.
  GAP-B8 records real I2C timeouts on this board, so the trigger exists. Fix:
  one bounded DDC attempt with no sleep, or schedule the retry from a work item
  and return -EAGAIN/unknown to the probe.
- E2 [minor, major upstream] The EDID is doctored. The read buffer is fixed at
  256 bytes (SM750_DRM_EDID_SIZE at :104, kmalloc(256) at :1088), and when the
  sink advertises more than one extension block the driver forces
  edid->extensions = 1 and recomputes the checksum (:1113-1121) before
  publishing the blob at :1129. Userspace - including edid-decode via the
  connector sysfs blob and the compositor's capability parsing - therefore
  receives an EDID the sink never sent, with second and later extension blocks
  silently dropped (CEA is usually block 1, so most HDMI capability survives;
  DisplayID/HDMI-Forum blocks in later blocks do not). The same 256-byte cap
  drives the changed-monitor heuristic, which memcmp()s only 256 bytes
  (connected_edid[256] at :411, :1004) [S]. Fix: use drm_edid_read_ddc() and
  publish the unmodified blob.
- E3 [minor] sm750_connector_get_modes rewrites connector->probed_modes after
  the fact (sm750_remove_probed_modes, hotplug-preferred overrides at
  :1187-1199). It works because it happens inside the probe call, but it is
  unusual and upstream would not accept driver-side mode list surgery.
- E4 [info] A hardcoded mode catalog used for a serial-link sink is a fallback
  for a broken DDC path, not a mode policy. It is harmless when EDID works and
  the default (edid_only=1) keeps it out of the way, but it should not be
  presented as a feature.

4. POWER MANAGEMENT
Score 3/10 - a suspend/resume handler exists, is structurally plausible, and
has never been exercised in any captured evidence.
===============================================================

- sm750_pm_suspend (:5324-5338) calls drm_mode_config_helper_suspend, stops the
  synthetic vblank timer with wait=true, and shuts the SiI902x down under
  mode_lock. sm750_pm_resume (:5340-5356) calls sm750_hw_init(sdev,false),
  sii902x_prepare and drm_mode_config_helper_resume [S]. The shape is right for
  a modeset-preserving suspend.
- No runtime PM: no pm_runtime_get/put, no autosuspend, no pci_set_power_state,
  no pci_enable_wake (grep over src/). For a soldered industrial part this is a
  defensible omission, but it is a deliberate absence, not an oversight [S].
- No pci_save_state/pci_restore_state: the PCI core restores config space for
  system sleep, and the driver reprograms all its own registers from
  sm750_hw_init, so this is not itself a bug [S/K].
- P1 [major] No evidence that suspend/resume has ever been run.
  docs/evidence/runtime/22-restore-fix-hardware-proof.txt:31 and :203 state
  explicitly "No suspend/resume cycle in this session" and "nothing is claimed
  about the restore". The path is therefore unverified code in the production
  binary. Either test it (rtcwake S3 cycle with a monitor attached, then check
  PANEL_CURRENT_LINE, a fresh modeset and one shadow upload) or delete the PM
  ops so the kernel cannot enter a state the driver has never seen.
- P2 [minor, unverified] DMA interrupt bookkeeping across resume.
  ddk750_init_hw (src/ddk750_chip.c:213-300) resets clocks and aborts DMA1
  (DMA_ABORT_INTERRUPT_ABORT_1) but never writes INT_MASK (no INT_MASK
  reference exists anywhere in ddk750_*.c). sdev->dma_irq_enabled is not
  cleared by sm750_pm_resume, so after a resume the driver believes the DMA
  interrupt is unmasked. If the chip's INT_MASK does not survive D3, the first
  post-resume upload times out and sm750_dma_wait_active sets the sticky
  shadow_dma_broken flag (:1623-1630) -> CPU copies for the rest of the boot.
  Impact is performance only. Verification: read INT_MASK after a resume, or
  run one S3 cycle with the debug counter for DMA fallbacks enabled.
- P3 [info] sm750_pci_shutdown (:5315) does atomic_helper_shutdown plus vblank
  stop but no sii902x_shutdown; a kexec or reboot leaves the transmitter
  powered with the link up. Cosmetic, but upstream would ask for symmetry with
  remove.

5. HOTPLUG, REMOVAL AND PROBE UNWINDING
Score 8/10 - the best-engineered part of the driver: devres covers every
resource, remove is ordered and idempotent, and the A52 observation is
explained without a driver leak.
===============================================================

- Probe (:4998-5290) takes regions, enables the device with pcim_enable_device
  (:5031), sets the DMA mask (dma_set_mask_and_coherent(DMA_BIT_MASK(31)) at
  :1769), maps BAR1 registers and BAR0 WC VRAM with devm unmap actions, adds a
  devm workqueue action, adds a devm sysfs group, registers, then starts
  polling. Error paths use standard goto labels; every allocation has a devm or
  drmm owner [S].
- Remove (:5300-5313): poll_fini, drm_dev_unplug, test-pattern cancel,
  drm_atomic_helper_shutdown, vblank timer stop with wait=true, mode_lock +
  sii902x_shutdown. drm_dev_unplug unregisters and marks the device unplugged,
  which makes all subsequent ioctls fail with ENODEV; the final drm_dev_put is
  owned by devm_drm_dev_alloc. There is no manual drm_dev_unregister and no
  get/put asymmetry [S].
- R1 [info] A52 (module refcount 1 after a full PCI unbind) is consistent with
  a userspace reference, not a driver leak: DEFINE_DRM_GEM_FOPS sets
  .owner = THIS_MODULE (drm_gem.h:489-493 [K]), so any open card or render fd
  pins the module while the compositor keeps it open. This matches "transient
  and not schedulable" and needs no code change; closing the last fd drops it.
- R2 [minor] The sysfs attribute group is added with devm_device_add_group
  (:5259). devm removes the group at detach, before the register mapping is
  unmapped (LIFO order), so the window is narrow, but a thread that already
  holds a kernfs reference can still enter a show callback after unmap. Upstream
  prefers explicit device_remove_groups() in remove. Verification: a race test
  that reads the sysfs attrs in a loop while unbinding.
- R3 [minor] sm750_pipe_disable (:4247) and the fail path of pipe_enable both
  run sii902x_shutdown/disable_link under mode_lock, while remove calls
  sii902x_shutdown again under the same lock. Idempotent in practice, but the
  removal path is the only place that knows the ordering against
  drm_atomic_helper_shutdown; a comment would make it reviewable.

6. CONCURRENCY ARCHITECTURE (design level, not the line audit)
Score 6/10 - there is a single documented ownership model for hardware access
(mode_lock) and separate locks for the data paths, but four private mutexes, an
hrtimer, a vblank work and an ordered workqueue is more machinery than a
single-pipe driver should need, and the lock/IRQ boundary is only explained in
comments scattered across the file.
===============================================================

- Ownership: mode_lock serialises all register-level modeset work (enable,
  disable, detect, PM, SiI902x bring-up); shadow_lock serialises scanout
  uploads and the damage queue; cursor_lock guards the cursor plane; async_lock
  guards the async-update queue. vblank_lock (spinlock) guards the hrtimer;
  crc_work is a drm_vblank_work. Hardware access from the atomic commit path,
  the workqueue and the timer is therefore split by subsystem rather than by
  one rule [S].
- C1 [minor] The vblank event path takes dev->event_lock and then calls
  drm_crtc_vblank_get() and drm_crtc_arm_vblank_event()
  (src/sm750_drm.c:3070-3087). Whether holding event_lock across those two
  calls is permitted depends on core contracts that cannot be read from this
  trimmed kernel tree (kernel-devel ships headers only, and drm_vblank.h:294-303
  gives prototypes without kerneldoc). The live session proves it does not
  self-deadlock today; the open question is lock ordering against
  drm_crtc_handle_vblank, which runs from the hrtimer softirq. Verification:
  read drivers/gpu/drm/drm_vblank.c at the matching tag, or run a vblank-event
  stress (modetest -v in a loop plus commits) under CONFIG_PROVE_LOCKING.
  Flagged for the line-level reviewer, not asserted as a defect.
- C2 [minor] The synthetic vblank timer runs in hardirq/softirq context and
  takes vblank_lock; sm750_vblank_timer_stop uses try_to_cancel under that lock
  with an optional wait (:513-527), which is a pattern that needs the wait=true
  call sites to be outside atomic context (they are: remove and PM suspend)
  [S].
- C3 [info] The model is documented but not centralised: the rules live in
  comments at :432-436, :2394-2415, :4198-4206 and :4780-4790 rather than in a
  header block. For a 5380-line file that is the difference between "hard" and
  "impossible" to review.

7. UPSTREAMABILITY
Score 3/10 - the display part is close to a drivers/gpu/drm/tiny/ driver, but
the vendor port, the private bridge driver and 33 module parameters would be
rejected before anyone read the mode logic.
===============================================================

Rejected out of hand by drm-misc or staging:
- U1 [major] The ddk750_* vendor port (10 files, ~2100 lines) with vendor
  naming, vendor register macros and a global register base (mmio750 in
  src/ddk750_chip.h:27-34) is not acceptable as-is. drm-misc would ask for a
  ~1500-line driver written against drm, not a port of the SMI SDK.
- U2 [major] The private SiI902x TPI implementation (src/sii902x.c, 1274 lines)
  duplicates drivers/gpu/drm/bridge/sii902x.c (external knowledge; verify
  against a full upstream tree). It registers no i2c adapter and no
  drm_bridge; the CRTC calls into it directly. The acceptable shape is: expose
  the SM750's bit-banged/hardware I2C as an i2c_adapter, instantiate the
  mainline bridge, and attach it with drm_simple_display_pipe_attach_bridge.
  This is also the hardest single piece of work in the list.
- U3 [major] 33 module parameters (24 in src/sm750_drm.c, 9 in src/sii902x.c),
  almost all 0444 hardware knobs; four are 0644 writable at runtime
  (dma_batch_rows, staging_timing, crc_window_bytes, test_pattern*). Upstream
  policy: no module parameters for normal operation; debug knobs go to debugfs.
- U4 [minor] CFLAGS_sm750_dither.o += -O3 (src/Makefile:6) and environment
  variable selected -D defaults (src/Makefile:7-18). Non-standard kbuild and
  untestable in CI.
- U5 [minor] src/Makefile is a module-only kbuild file (obj-m). An in-tree
  submission needs drivers/gpu/drm/tiny/Kconfig + Makefile entries and a
  MAINTAINERS record; neither exists in this tree.
- U6 [minor] 14 LINUX_VERSION_CODE uses and 13 lines over 100 columns (no //
  comments exist in src/, so that part of the coding style is clean). Fixable
  in an afternoon.
- U7 [clean] Licensing and metadata: SPDX GPL-2.0 in every file, MODULE_LICENSE
  ("GPL v2"), MODULE_AUTHOR, MODULE_DESCRIPTION, MODULE_VERSION and
  MODULE_DEVICE_TABLE(pci, ...) present (:5361-5377) [S]. The ddk750 headers
  carry "Copyright (c) 2007 by Silicon Motion, Inc. (SMI)" plus the GPL-2.0
  SPDX tag; no conflicting proprietary notice was found. This is not what
  blocks a submission.
- U8 [info] The brief's premise that ddk750_reg.h contains IOCTL_* vendor
  headers is false: ddk750_reg.h is 1456 lines of plain #defines with an
  SPDX tag and zero IOCTL_ macros [S]. The vendor problem is the file's naming
  and the register-level abstraction, not a copied Windows header.
- U9 [good] Pure PCI bind (pci_device_id table at :5361, module_pci_driver at
  :5378, no OF match). For a PCI card with an on-board bridge that is the
  correct bind; a DT binding is not needed. drivers/gpu/drm/tiny/ is the right
  home (simple pipe, shadow plane, drm_client_setup), not staging.
- U10 [minor] Description vs silicon: MODULE_DESCRIPTION says "SM750G10
  SiI9024A", the project brief says SiI9022A; both parts share the TPI register
  map, so this is a naming inconsistency only [S/U].

Genuinely hard to fix, in order:
  1. SiI902x: replace the private TPI driver with an i2c_adapter + the mainline
     bridge (U2).
  2. The DDK port: rewrite as a small register-access layer with per-device
     state (U1, F6).
  3. The dual driver personality (F7): pick GEM SHMEM + shadow plane and delete
     the TTM/VRAM personality.

===============================================================
8. CODE STRUCTURE AND MAINTAINABILITY
Score 5/10 - the layer boundaries are real (policy / DDK / bridge / dither) but
the policy file carries two drivers, 24 parameters and the sysfs surface, and
one helper is dead code.
===============================================================

- sm750_drm.c 5380 lines holds: DRM objects, atomic callbacks, the shadow
  DMA engine, the synthetic vblank, CRC, the test pattern, sysfs and debugfs,
  and both drm_driver structs. Splitting it (sm750_vblank.c, sm750_shadow.c,
  sm750_sysfs.c) is mechanical and would make section 6 reviewable.
- ddk750_* (~2100 lines) is a vendor port with global state; sm750_dither.c
  (609) implements the panel's RGB565 dithering, which is hardware-driven and
  justified; sm750_drm_mode.c (79) is a small mode conversion helper.
- F1 [minor] Duplication across the two personalities: sm750_pipe_funcs
  (:4295-4307) and sm750_shadow_pipe_funcs (:4309-4321) differ in two members
  (prepare_fb and the shadow-plane macro) but are otherwise copied; the two
  drm_driver structs (:4537-4590) and the two mode_config_funcs
  (:4350-4361) are likewise near-copies. Branching on a load-time constant in
  every path (13+ sites) is what makes the file hard to follow.
- F2 [minor] Dead code: sm750_mode_from_fb (src/sm750_drm_mode.c:50-79,
  declared in sm750_drm_mode.h:29) has no caller in src/ (the only call is
  sm750_mode_from_drm at src/sm750_drm.c:1393), and it drags
  #include <linux/fb.h> into an otherwise DRM-only helper. Left over from an
  fbdev entry point that no longer exists.
- F3 [info] The shadow-scanout machinery is justified on real silicon: the
  SM750 display engine scans from VRAM with pitch/offset constraints, so
  damage-clipped copies into a VRAM shadow are the normal tiny-driver answer
  (the same shape as mgag200's shadow). The double-buffer/async/DMA staging
  layer on top of it is an optimisation, not a requirement, and it is the part
  that carries the most state.
- F4 [info] sm750_scanout_offset (:3057) assumes the fb's object is a
  drm_gem_vram_object (drm_gem_vram_of_gem). That is true for the TTM
  personality only; the function is unreachable in the shmem personality
  because the shadow branch is taken instead (:4014/:4067). A reader cannot
  tell that without following both branches.

===============================================================
9. TESTABILITY AND OBSERVABILITY
Score 6/10 - an unusually good debug surface for a tiny driver (register dump,
scanout readback with CRC, sysfs link/vblank counters), but nothing that
observes a failed mode set at the UAPI level, and no automated tests in-tree.
===============================================================

- debugfs: "regs" (bounded read-only register dump) and "sm750_scanout"
  (bounded VRAM readback with a CRC32 summary) plus a crc/control file on the
  >=7.0 path [S].
- sysfs on the PCI device: hpd, rxsense, link_state, active_crtc,
  vblank_count, crc_supported, vram totals (devm_device_add_group at :5259)
  [S]. This is what a field engineer needs.
- CRC path: drm_vblank_work based scanout CRC, gated on kernel >= 7.0
  (SM750_CRC_SUPPORTED), reading the scanout window directly rather than the
  DAC output (:3249-3344) [S]. Honest about what it measures.
- Missing: no tracepoints (drm has drm_vblank_event and trace_drm_* already;
  a couple of tracepoints around mode set and DMA fallback would close the
  field-diagnosis gap), no KUnit, no IGT wiring, no fault injection.
- T1 [major] The failed mode set is invisible to userspace (A1). A field log
  shows one drm_err line; the compositor shows a black screen and no error.
  Adding a counter plus a sysfs file (and a drm_dbg on the successful path)
  would let a support engineer distinguish "panel dark" from "no mode".

===============================================================
10. RISKS THAT WOULD BITE IN PRODUCTION AND NO EXISTING GATE CATCHES
===============================================================

- X1 [major] The 3 s EDID sleep (E1) with no test that exercises a failing DDC.
- X2 [major] Suspend/resume is shipped untested (P1); a single S3 cycle on a
  scratch machine would settle it.
- X3 [major] Two SM750 cards in one machine: the second probe overwrites the
  global mmio750 (src/sm750_drm.c:4578, ddk750_chip.h:27-34) and both devices
  then program the same card. No gate tests two cards; the fix is per-device
  state in the DDK layer.
- X4 [major] A failed enable reports success (A1); GAP-B8 proves it happens.
- X5 [minor] Synthetic vblank drift: the timer free-runs at frame_ns derived
  from the requested mode clock and resyncs from PANEL_CURRENT_LINE only when
  re-enabled (:443-489). Over a long uptime, vblank timestamps drift against
  the real PLL, which affects frame pacing but not correctness. The 7.3.4
  kernel offers drm_vblank_crtc_timer (drm_vblank.h:110-127) and
  drm_crtc_next_vblank_start (:293), so this is now re-implementing core.
- X6 [minor] Sticky DMA fallback (P2): one timeout permanently switches to CPU
  copies until reload; a performance cliff with a single log line.
- X7 [info] DRIVER_RENDER with no Mesa driver: the render node works but
  renders on llvmpipe. It is a capability claim the driver cannot accelerate;
  harmless, but do not count it as an acceleration feature.

===============================================================
CLAIMS CHECKED AND REFUTED
===============================================================

- "Enabling the CRTC with the primary plane disabled causes a NULL dereference
  in sm750_pipe_enable." Not reachable. sm750_pipe_enable does dereference
  plane_state->fb unconditionally at :4070 and :4073, and via
  sm750_shadow_rect (:4067) -> sm750_shadow_rect_locked (:2402), while the same
  function guards another fb use at :4036. But the simple-pipe helper's CRTC
  atomic_check (drm_simple_kms_crtc_check, System.map:112518) rejects an
  enabled CRTC without an enabled primary plane, and that callback lives in
  drm_crtc_helper_funcs (crtc->helper_private), which the driver never
  overrides [K/S]. Retained as a robustness finding only: the driver's own
  check (sm750_pipe_check, :3038-3052) does not cover the case, so the safety
  depends on a helper it does not control. Recommended hardening: return
  -EINVAL from a driver-supplied check when crtc_state->enable &&
  !plane_state->fb, and guard the two dereferences.
- "Installing sm750_crtc_funcs drops the helper's atomic_check." False for the
  same reason: atomic_check is not a drm_crtc_funcs member
  (drm_crtc.h, struct drm_crtc_funcs has no atomic_check) and the driver does
  not touch helper_private.
- "The binary uses IOCTL_* vendor headers." False; ddk750_reg.h has none.
