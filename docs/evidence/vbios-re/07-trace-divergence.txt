Divergence detection: one flipped instruction byte, T25

The comparison must report a difference instead of reporting success, and it
must not shorten the bound until the difference disappears. This run flips
one byte of the captured image in a scratch copy - offset 0x125a, the second
instruction after the entry point: 0x1e push ds becomes 0x06 push es - and
compares it against the captured image itself.

--- 1. build the mutated copy ---
$ cp /mega/tmp/sm750/vbios-01-00-0.bin /mega/tmp/sm750/tr/mut.bin

$ printf '\x06' | dd of=/mega/tmp/sm750/tr/mut.bin bs=1 seek=$((0x125a)) conv=notrunc

1+0 records out
1 byte copied, 0.000249833 s, 4.0 kB/s
