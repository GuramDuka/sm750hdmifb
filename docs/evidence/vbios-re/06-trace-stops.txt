Stop rules, skip paths and the port collision, T25

Every command runs from the repository root. The synthetic images are two
scratch files of 0x125b bytes, not in the tree: their bytes at offset 0x1256
are the whole program, because the harness starts execution there. Cases 6
to 8 therefore cannot satisfy the real-image acceptance checks (at least 100
steps, entry into 0xc6065) and exit 1; what those cases test is the stop
reason and the step count each engine reports.

--- 1. the captured image is absent ---
$ env VBIOS_ROM=/nonexistent/rom.bin ./tools/vbios-re/trace.sh
SKIP: captured ROM not present at /nonexistent/rom.bin
exit=77

--- 2. qemu-system-i386 is absent (PATH holds only sh, dirname, python3, env) ---
$ env PATH=/mega/tmp/sm750/tr/p1 ./tools/vbios-re/trace.sh
SKIP: qemu-system-i386 not installed
exit=77

--- 3. gdb is absent (same PATH plus qemu-system-i386) ---
$ env PATH=/mega/tmp/sm750/tr/p2 ./tools/vbios-re/trace.sh
SKIP: gdb not installed
exit=77

--- 4. capstone is absent (no helper prefix, empty PYTHONPATH) ---
$ env PYTHONPATH= VBIOS_PYLIB=/nonexistent ./tools/vbios-re/trace.sh
SKIP: python3 capstone not importable (VBIOS_PYLIB=/nonexistent)
exit=77

--- 5. the gdb port is already in use ---
