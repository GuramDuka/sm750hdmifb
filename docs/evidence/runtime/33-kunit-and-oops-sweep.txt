## LIVE SWEEP COMPLETED, KUNIT ITSELF DISABLED

The sweep is no longer unproven at the gate level: all 21 allowlisted suites
were loaded and unloaded for real on this host, and the gate discovered,
parsed, unmapped and reported their output. What is unproven is KUnit's own
execution, and the reason is environmental, not a bug in the gate:

  - /boot/config-7.3.4-100.fc43.x86_64 has
    "# CONFIG_KUNIT_DEFAULT_ENABLED is not set", so kunit loads with
    enable=0 even though CONFIG_KUNIT_AUTORUN_ENABLED=y.
  - Consequently each suite prints "KTAP version 1 / 1..1 /
    kunit: disabled" and no subtest, and the gate skips with exit 77 naming
    that reason instead of reporting a pass.

Verified on this host:
  - module discovery and the floor (23 .ko.xz files, floor 8);
  - plain modprobe resolution through modules.dep;
  - the live allowlisted loader path (MODPROBE_CMD='sudo -n /usr/bin/modprobe')
    for all 21 suites, with reverse unloads on every exit path;
  - the TAP parser against synthetic logs (four planted defects rejected:
    failing_subtest, missing_suite, missing_totals, totals_mismatch; two
    negative controls accepted: clean_run, skipped_subtest);
  - the oops detector end to end against the live allowlisted
    'sudo -n dmesg' reader, wrapping tests/check-drm-policy.sh and
    tests/check-sm750-pkg.sh, both clean.
