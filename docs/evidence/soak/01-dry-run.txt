Fields that are unavailable on this tree, and why
------------------------------------------------
b8_* counters (11 cells read "-" in every sample)
  The loaded module is 0.6.10, the committed tree. The eleven b8_* attributes
  are part of the GAP-B8 work that is still uncommitted (another task's edits
  to src/sm750_drm.c and tests/check-sysfs-attributes.sh), so sysfs does not
  export them. The counters-monotonic criterion therefore passed with nothing
  to check: it is a PASS with no evidence, and the report must not read it as
  counter evidence. Once the GAP-B8 attributes are loaded, the same helper
  samples them with no change.

crc ("unavailable:probe-only-supported=1")
  The only allowlisted CRC entry point is `sudo -n /usr/local/sbin/sm750-crc`
  with no argument, which runs its read-only `probe` subcommand:
    --- crc/source (supported sources)
    ... one failing cat, so the probe exits 1 ...
  It reports that the source exists (crc_supported=1) and that CRC is
  supported, but it prints no value. Producing a crc needs `run`, which writes
  the debugfs control, and a write is what this helper refuses to do. The cell
  states the reason instead of inventing a number.

gates ("unavailable:no-gate-log")
  No gate sweep had been recorded at --gates (default
  /mega/tmp/sm750/soak/last-gate-results.txt) at sampling time, so every
  sample recorded the missing measurement. For the real 24 h run the owner
  should write a sweep there:
    ./tests/check-drm-policy.sh >/mega/tmp/sm750/soak/last-gate-results.txt 2>&1

vblank_count frozen at 7056 for the whole window
  This is a recorded observation, not a failure, and the helper says so in the
  criterion line. The pipe was idle: DRM core freezes vblank_count after
  vblankoffdelay when nothing is waiting for vblanks
  (docs/evidence/runtime/34-vblank-idle-freeze). The panel was demonstrably
  alive by a different signal: PANEL_CURRENT_LINE took six distinct values,
  0x00000209, 0x00000089, 0x000000f0, 0x00000097, 0x000001be and 0x00000314.
  That is what the panel-vblank criterion requires: vblank present and never
  decreasing, plus the scanout pointer moving. Had the panel pointer stopped
  too, panel-vblank would have failed.

  criterion for a dry run.

What 5 minutes does not prove
-----------------------------
- Nothing about 24 or 48 h. Six samples cover 300 s. A leak, a slow reference
  imbalance, a thermal fault or a fault that needs a mode change cannot appear
  in a five-minute window; the run's own summary says so.
- Nothing about the b8_* counters, as recorded above: the attributes are not in
  the loaded module.
- Nothing about a crc, for the read-only reason above.
- Nothing about the gate results of the release, since no gate log was
  recorded at sampling time.
- Nothing about a mode set, a hot reload, a suspend/resume, heavy use, or the
  CRT-only output path. The soak only observes.
- Nothing about vblank progress: vblank_count was frozen by an idle pipe for
  all six samples.
